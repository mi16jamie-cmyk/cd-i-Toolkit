MEGARACE FOR CD-i - READ ME
===========================

Current version:1.1beta (18 Oct 2026)

Controls: the pad steers (pull back to brake) and moves through the menus; button 1 accelerates , button 2 fires, both buttons together do both. The buttons can be swapped, and accelerating made automatic, in SETTINGS.

Title menu: START GAME, LOAD GAME, SETTINGS (difficulty, race music, sound effects, buttons, accelerate), WATCH THE INTRO, CREDITS.

Race panel: green = start race, yellow = choose car, blue = options (and QUIT GAME), red = save / load. The windows show the cars you can drive.

Weapons and racing
- in easy mode unlimited ammunition; shots lock on to an opponent in sight and always hit.
- lock-on and hits only count when the opponent is in range, so fights are a bit harder.
- the road markings coming up are shown on the dashboard screen (icons for the next few frames); the name of the marking driven over (TURBO, BONUS...) shows briefly at the top.
- the player's shot is no longer drawn sitting on the car when it fires.
- the track runs a third faster (20 frames a second instead of 15), so laps are about a quarter quicker.

Cars
- fix for Maria's car: her wheels and gauges used the wrong pictures in race13-16.
- the panel windows show the unlocked cars (the others keep the VW logo); the chosen car's light bar is lit. As in the PC game: JOSE, LUIS and OUZBEL at the start; winning a race with every opponent destroyed unlocks that gang's car (RAMON, MARIA, HOOPER, OMEGA, PALOMA). The car select skips locked cars.

Screens and menus
- a car picture added to the dashboard screen, then taken out again to match the original.
-  fix: the dashboard no longer wipes visibly for a frame at the end of a race or when a menu picture loads.
-  title menu with LOAD GAME and SETTINGS (difficulty easy/normal/hard, race music on/off, sound effects on/off, swap buttons, automatic accelerate). Save / load game; saves, settings and the best score are kept in the CD-i's built-in memory (NVRAM).
-  the race panel has four buttons: start, choose car, options (with QUIT GAME), save / load (new red button).
-  new title screen: a car on a looping track with a chrome MEGARACE logo.
-  a LOADING box between START RACE and the race.

Sound and video
-  race music: each level's own song from the PC game, mixed with the sound effects (played by the cartridge's MPEG audio decoder).
- the music louder (about +10%, then +9% more).
- fix: Lance's clip before each race (B64) was a still picture with almost no speech; it now plays in full.

Speed
- loading before a race about 11 s -> 6 s (race's files are packed into two).
